# Procurement Mind Map (2026 Perspective)

This repository contains a mind map of concepts around fair, effective, transparent, and equitable procurement, specifically geared towards government and the year 2026.

## High-Level Principles

Modern government procurement is built on a foundation of integrity and value:

- **Fairness & Competition**: Ensuring a level playing field where innovation can thrive.
- **Transparency & Accountability**: Proactive disclosure of data across the entire lifecycle.
- **Effectiveness & Outcome-Focus**: Delivering real-world results rather than just checking compliance boxes.
- **Professionalization**: Equipping the workforce with specialized digital skills and agile methodologies.

## Digital Procurement & Professionalization

Digital procurement requires a specialized workforce and an agile mindset:

- **Modular Contracting**: Acquiring systems in successive, interoperable increments (FAR Part 39).
- **FAC-C-Digital Services (FAC-C-DS)**: The professional specialization for contracting officers working in digital.
- **DITAP**: The premier training program for becoming a digital procurement "change ambassador."
- **TechFAR Hub**: The US federal portal for agile acquisition, covering the full lifecycle from pre-solicitation to contract administration.
