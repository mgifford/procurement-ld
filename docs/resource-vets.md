# Resource Reliability & Vetting

This document evaluates the reliability and authority of the resources used to build this procurement mind map.

## Tier 1: Authoritative Government Sources
These are official repositories of law, regulation, and policy. They are highly reliable for defining the "Rules of the Game."

- **Gov.uk (UK)**: Official portal for UK public sector procurement policy. Managed by the Crown Commercial Service (CCS). Includes the **Procurement Act 2023** and **Technology Code of Practice**.
- **Acquisition.gov (USA)**: Official home of the **Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR)** and the **Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO)**. Managed by GSA.
- **TechFAR Hub (USA)**: Official USDS/OMB resource for agile digital procurement guidance.
- **FAI.gov (USA)**: Federal Acquisition Institute; authoritative for contracting certifications and professionalization (FAC-C-DS).
- **NIAA (Australia)**: National Indigenous Australians Agency; official source for the Indigenous Procurement Policy.

## Tier 2: International Organizations & Frameworks
These organizations set global benchmarks and standards for transparency and effectiveness.

- **Open Contracting Partnership (OCP)**: Global leader in procurement data standards (OCDS) and anti-corruption frameworks. Widely cited by governments (UK, Mexico, etc.).
- **World Bank (IEG)**: Independent Evaluation Group; provides high-quality research on the effectiveness of global procurement reforms.
- **OECD**: Sets the "Principles for Public Procurement" used by most developed nations.

## Tier 3: Industry Thought Leaders & Specialists
These resources provide practical "on-the-ground" insights and modern framing that go beyond strict legal text.

- **Procurement with Purpose (Peter Smith)**: Highly regarded book and framework for using spend to drive social and environmental outcomes. Smith is a Fellow of CIPS.
- **Posterity Global**: Specialists in user-centered digital procurement. They work directly with UK and international government agencies.
- **TandemGov**: Experts in US government contracting strategy and agile acquisition coaching.
- **PQAR**: A global reference point for procurement quality assessment.

## Tier 4: Industry News & Blogs
Useful for identifying current trends and practitioner "calling cards," but should be cross-referenced with Tier 1 sources.

- **Graphite Connect**: Strategic sourcing and supplier management insights.
- **Tracker Intelligence**: UK-focused procurement news and tender alerts.
- **Spendflo**: Insights into SaaS procurement and digital spend optimization.
